Where AI fits

Part of the workflow.
Not a substitute for it.

SGJ uses AI across several different kinds of work. The role changes depending on the task: sometimes it is a development partner, sometimes a production assistant, and occasionally it is deliberately given control inside a gameplay experiment.

DEV

Mods & software development

AI supports architecture work, debugging, implementation, code review, compatibility analysis, test planning and documentation across interconnected Farming Simulator mods, utilities and companion applications.

The important part is the loop: generate or analyse, test against the real project, inspect the result, then iterate.

CONTENT

Content & production

AI is used to explore concepts, structure series, develop titles and hooks, organise research, refine descriptions and work through production decisions without handing over the SGJ voice or editorial judgement.

An idea can come from AI. Whether it fits SimGamerJen is still a human decision.

VISUAL

Visual concepts & assets

AI can help develop thumbnails, overlays, graphic concepts and visual directions. Outputs are reviewed and adjusted for the actual use case, and platform or submission rules take priority where AI-generated artwork is not permitted.

The aim is useful creative acceleration, not pretending generated work appeared from nowhere.

WEB

Website & project operations

AI assists with site design, implementation planning, code changes, documentation, security analysis and project organisation. Changes still go through the same practical controls: branches, previews, testing and deliberate merges.

A fast answer is not the same thing as a safe change.

A different use of AI

When AI is deliberately
given the keys.

AI-directed gameplay is a separate idea from AI-assisted production. In the Cline Ridge Farm series, the AI owner is intentionally given decision authority inside the rules of the save while the player carries those decisions out in Farming Simulator 25.

That tension is the point of the experiment: the AI is not quietly helping behind the scenes; its decisions become part of the story and can succeed, fail or create consequences that have to be dealt with in-game.

Working method

AI output is a starting point.
Evidence closes the loop.

The exact workflow varies by project, but the principle is consistent: AI can accelerate thinking and execution, while the real environment decides whether the result is actually good enough.

1

Define the job

Start with the real objective, constraints and existing project state rather than asking for generic output.

2

Explore or build

Use AI to analyse options, generate a first implementation, identify risks or propose a testable direction.

3

Validate

Test in FS25, inspect logs, run the application, preview the website, compare against APIs or verify the source material.

4

Make the human call

Keep, revise or reject the result. Releases, publishing decisions and accountability remain with SGJ.

Models & tools

No single model does every job.

SGJ is not tied to the idea that one AI model should handle everything. Architecture, debugging and difficult technical decisions may benefit from deeper reasoning, while routine production or transformation work may be better handled by faster tools.

The exact models will change over time. The useful principle is to match capability to the task rather than treat the newest or largest model as the automatic answer.

Limitations matter

AI can be confidently wrong.

It can invent APIs, misunderstand game behaviour, produce fragile code, miss project context or suggest a creative direction that simply does not fit.

That is why SGJ treats verification as part of the workflow rather than an optional final check.

Transparency

Different roles deserve
different labels.

There is a meaningful difference between AI helping to build something and AI being the premise of the thing itself. SGJ aims to make that distinction clear where it matters to the audience.

AI-ASSISTED

AI contributes to research, development, planning or production, while SGJ retains control of the result.

AI-DIRECTED

AI is intentionally given defined decision authority as part of the content or experiment.

AI-GENERATED VISUAL

AI materially creates a visual asset or concept used in the work, subject to the rules of the platform where it appears.
