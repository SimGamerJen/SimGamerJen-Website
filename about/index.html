About SimGamerJen

Serious about simulation.
Not always the result.

SimGamerJen is built around simulation games, long-form worlds, livestreaming, experimentation and practical mod development — with fun kept ahead of perfection.

The brand

Fun first. Skills later.

The channel follows simulation worlds rather than treating every upload as a disconnected challenge. Farms develop, cities grow, trucks keep moving and mistakes become part of the story.

Alongside the content, SGJ develops Farming Simulator mods and utilities aimed at realism and quality of life: more believable workers, better farm-management decisions and more control over how a save behaves.

The aim of this website is to bring those strands together without replacing the platforms that already do their jobs well. YouTube and Twitch host the content. GitHub hosts the development work. SimGamerJen.com is the front door.
