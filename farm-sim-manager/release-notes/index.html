Release notes

0.1.0-beta.1
Community Preview.

The first limited preview of the launcher, mod-management, save/profile and diagnostics foundation.

Included

What shipped.

  • FS25 user-data discovery, including custom locations and mod-directory overrides.
  • Named mod collections with browse/register/rename/activate workflows.
  • ZIP and unpacked mod inventory and metadata inspection.
  • Verified mod source associations and version checks.
  • Savegame inspection and recorded mod requirements.
  • Mod Folder ↔ Mod Folder, Savegame ↔ Savegame and Savegame ↔ Mod Folder comparison.
  • Launch profiles associating savegames with registered mod collections.
  • Guarded gameSettings.xml switching with backup, validation and recovery.
  • Pre-launch profile/save/mod health checking and FS25 launch lifecycle feedback.
  • FS25/FSM log monitoring and support-bundle export.

Not yet

Deliberately outside Beta 0.1.

  • Production New Career/save generation.
  • First-run CCO/bootstrap integration.
  • Automatic mod download/update/install.
  • Commercial updater/licensing infrastructure.
  • Advanced multi-save fault-isolation tooling.
Important beta warning: back up important FS25 savegames, settings and custom mod-folder configuration before testing. Current binaries are not Authenticode-signed and may trigger SmartScreen “Unknown publisher” warnings.
