CCOCROP CONTROL OVERRIDE

What it does

Control what grows — and what the world does next.

Crop Control Override is designed to give a save more deliberate regional and gameplay rules. It can control crop availability, rebuild NPC field states and support crop-calendar behaviour rather than leaving every map to a single generic setup.

Crop controlEnable or disable crops to suit the map, region or play style.
NPC field regenerationRebuild field states so the surrounding world follows the chosen crop rules.
Crop calendarsSupport regional growing windows and seasonal behaviour.
Save-focused designRules can be used as part of an ongoing save rather than only at setup time.
