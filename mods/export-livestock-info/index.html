What it does

Take the herd out of the XML.

ExportLivestockInfo is a Python utility rather than an in-game script mod. It reads livestock information from a Farming Simulator 25 save and converts it into data that is much easier to inspect, filter and analyse.

Animal exportCreates one row per animal with breed, sex, age, health, genetics, birthday, pregnancy and other save data.
Pregnancy detailExports fetus records separately and links them back to the mother, including calculated due dates.
Summary reportingBuilds aggregated herd statistics by shed, species and breed.
Excel or CSVWrites a three-sheet XLSX workbook when openpyxl is available, with CSV fallback when it is not.
Realistic Livestock supportCan map stored country IDs to country names and ISO codes using Realistic Livestock AREA_CODES.
