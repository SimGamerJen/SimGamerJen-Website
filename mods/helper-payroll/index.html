£HELPER PAYROLL

What it does

Labour should cost something.

HelperPayroll adds financial weight to hiring workers. It is designed to complement HelperProfiles so a persistent roster can also have meaningful labour costs, roles and payroll behaviour.

Payroll modelMove beyond simple vanilla helper charging toward deliberate labour costs.
Role-based labourDifferent worker roles can support different financial expectations.
Roster integrationDesigned to work with HelperProfiles so unavailable workers are not treated as active labour.
Farm-management pressureHiring help becomes a trade-off between time, capacity and cash.
