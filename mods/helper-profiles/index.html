HPHELPER PROFILES

What it does

Make hired workers part of the farm.

HelperProfiles gives the helper system a persistent roster layer. Instead of treating workers as interchangeable slots, the farm can maintain named profiles, control who is available and build a more believable labour setup around the save.

Worker rosterMaintain a persistent set of helper profiles rather than relying on anonymous workers.
AvailabilityToggle workers in and out of the active roster when they should or should not be hireable.
Roles and identityUse worker identity as a foundation for related labour and payroll systems.
World-worker directionOngoing development extends workers beyond normal AI jobs into the game world.
