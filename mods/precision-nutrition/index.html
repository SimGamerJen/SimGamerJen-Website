PNPRECISION NUTRITION

What it does

Make feeding decisions visible.

PrecisionNutrition adds a clearer management layer around livestock nutrition. It is aimed at players using deeper livestock systems who want feeding choices to be understandable and actionable in-game.

Nutrition visibilitySurface useful feeding information through an in-game management interface.
Livestock focusDesigned around more involved animal-management play rather than passive feeding.
Realism integrationBuilt with compatibility for realism-focused livestock workflows in mind.
Management UIUse dedicated controls to inspect and navigate nutrition information.
