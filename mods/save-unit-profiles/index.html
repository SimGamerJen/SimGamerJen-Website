SUPSAVE UNIT PROFILES

What it does

Let every map speak its own units.

SaveUnitProfiles adds a native Unit Profile selector under General Settings > Units. Choose a regional preset for the active save, or capture a custom setup, and the mod remembers that profile independently of your other Farming Simulator worlds.

Per-save profilesAssign a unit and currency profile to each savegame rather than relying on FS25’s global preferences.
Native settings UISelect profiles directly from General Settings > Units with contextual help describing each setup.
Regional presetsIncludes profiles for regions such as US, UK, EU, Canada, Brazil, Japan, Switzerland, Poland and more.
Custom profilesSave the current unit settings into a deterministic SAVEGAME_## profile unique to the active slot.
Standalone designNo dependency on Additional Currencies, Additional Game Settings or Realistic Livestock.
