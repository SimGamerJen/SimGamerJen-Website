Who operates this site

SimGamerJen (SGJ) is a UK-based creator brand and website for simulation gaming, livestreams, software projects and Farming Simulator modifications. This notice covers simgamerjen.com and its first-party pages and services. For questions about information processed through the site, contact simgamerjen@gmail.com.

This notice describes processing by the SimGamerJen site operator. Services that you open from this site, such as YouTube, Twitch and the merchandise store, have their own operators and privacy notices.

Information we use and why

Website requests and security

When you visit, hosting and security systems (including Cloudflare) may process technical information such as your IP address, request URL, browser or device information, access time and security signals. We use this to deliver the site, troubleshoot faults, maintain security and prevent misuse. Our legal basis is our legitimate interests in operating a reliable, secure website.

Messages and support requests

We do not currently provide a direct contact form on this website. If you email us or contact us through an external platform, we process the details you choose to send so we can respond and manage the enquiry. Our legal basis is our legitimate interests in responding to correspondence, or taking steps you request before entering an agreement where applicable.

Download statistics

The Farm Sim Manager download service records daily aggregate counts for a product, release version and download type in a Cloudflare D1 database. The counter itself does not store downloader names, email addresses, IP addresses or browser identifiers. Separate hosting and security request logs may still be processed as described above.

Public content

The site retrieves and displays channel information, video titles, thumbnails, publication dates, playlist information, community posts, and livestream details from SGJ's YouTube channels and Twitch. This lets visitors discover current content without creating an account on our website.

YouTube API Services and authorisation

SimGamerJen uses YouTube API Services and related publicly available YouTube feeds to show uploads, playlists, posts, scheduled streams and livestream status on this website. The site may access YouTube channel and video metadata, including information visible to the authorised channel operator, and temporarily cache responses to keep these features working.

For the two SGJ-owned channels, the operator grants permission using Google's youtube.readonly OAuth scope. This permits read-only access; the website does not use that permission to upload, modify, delete or publish YouTube content. OAuth refresh tokens are kept as restricted Cloudflare Worker secrets, and temporary access tokens may be cached by the server. We do not ask website visitors to sign in with Google or provide their Google credentials.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service. A channel owner who has authorised this application can revoke its Google access via Google Account third-party connections. To ask us about authorisation, cached API information or deletion of information we control, email simgamerjen@gmail.com.

We use the YouTube information to run SGJ's content displays and livestream features, not for targeted advertising or to sell account data.

External services and recipients

We use Cloudflare for hosting, delivery, security and server-side storage; Google/YouTube and Twitch to supply channel and livestream details; and GitHub to link to development projects and support. Links to Fourthwall (merchandise), Buy Me a Coffee (support), and social/community platforms lead to their own services. Those providers may process personal information according to their own notices when you interact with them.

Some providers operate internationally, so technical or account information may be processed outside the UK. Where we are responsible for an international transfer of personal data, applicable data-protection requirements and provider safeguards must be observed. Contact us if you need more information about a particular transfer.

Cookies, external media and similar technologies

The first-party website code inspected for this notice does not currently set optional advertising or analytics cookies. Cloudflare may use necessary security or traffic-management cookies where applicable. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are added later, we will update this notice and seek consent where required.

Video thumbnails and other media served by third parties can involve requests to their servers. On our homepage, the YouTube video player is inserted only when a visitor selects Play. Once you open a YouTube player or leave for YouTube, Twitch, the shop or another external site, that service may use its own cookies or similar technologies. See each provider's privacy and cookie information for details and controls.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, applicable obligations and legitimate dispute or security needs. Enquiry correspondence is reviewed and retained while necessary to address the enquiry and any related follow-up. Hosting and security records follow the retention settings and arrangements of the relevant service provider. Daily download totals are retained as non-identifying aggregate statistics; they do not identify individual downloaders.

We use access controls and service-provider safeguards appropriate to the information involved. No online service can guarantee absolute security. OAuth credentials are not placed into public website code or published repository files.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable UK data-protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict its use, object to certain processing, or request data portability. Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw it. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests; we will consider the request under the applicable rules.

For requests or questions, contact simgamerjen@gmail.com. If you remain dissatisfied, you can raise a concern with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when site features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes affecting how personal information is used will be communicated where the law requires.
